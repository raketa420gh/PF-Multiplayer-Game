# Teamplay

## Modes
- Solo
- Trio

## Parties
- Created in the tavern (Home tab), known by a four-digit code; others join by the code. Max 3.
- The creator is the leader: picks the queue and starts the run for everyone; members follow with the same ticket.
- A party of two may register for Trio; Solo takes no party ("switch to Trio or leave the party").
- After a run the party meets again in its tavern session; the first member back hosts it.
- Matchmaking details — `04_Multiplayer/Matchmaking.md`.

## Trio
- Max players: 3
- Spawn: teammates share a village house; every team gets its own house, at least 45 m from other teams while such houses are left.
- Deeper floors: a teammate who descends first holds the place — the rest of the party is matched into the same session.
